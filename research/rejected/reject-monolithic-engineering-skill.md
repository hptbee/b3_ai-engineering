# Reject — One giant engineering skill

## Considered

Single `software-engineering-everything` skill to reduce authoring burden.

## Evidence

- Superpowers/opposite: many composable skills (`obra-superpowers.md`)
- skill-creator / spec size limits (`anthropics-skills.md`, `agentskills-open-standard.md`)
- Anti-pattern entry (`anti-patterns.md`)

## Why rejected

Trigger imprecision; unmaintainable; eval surface impossible.

## Decision

**REJECT** monolithic catch-all skills.

## Alternative

Foundation engineering skills + future domain skills (current b3 approach).
