# Research

External research and synthesis **before** knowledge, skills, or rules are created.

**Status:** layout only. Do not add fake sources, findings, or decisions.

```text
External Sources
      ↓
   Research
      ↓
   Findings
      ↓
 Comparisons
      ↓
   Decisions
      ↓
 Knowledge
      ↓
 Skills / Rules / Workflows
```

Method: [`docs/research-methodology.md`](../docs/research-methodology.md). Promotion after that: [`docs/system-lifecycle.md`](../docs/system-lifecycle.md).

## Stores

| Store | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [`sources/`](sources/README.md) | What external projects, documentation, papers, or practices **say** |
| [`findings/`](findings/README.md) | Patterns **extracted** from those sources |
| [`comparisons/`](comparisons/README.md) | Evaluation of **multiple** approaches |
| [`decisions/`](decisions/README.md) | What we **adopted, modified, or rejected** |
| [`rejected/`](rejected/README.md) | Useful ideas we **intentionally did not adopt**, and why |

Research is not knowledge, not a skill reference, and not a profile. Do not copy third-party `SKILL.md` files here as if they were ours.

```text
knowledge  = what the system knows
reference  = material used to perform a task
research   = where conclusions came from
```
